Support

Common questions, answered.
How do I track an existing claim? ＋

Go to Claims from the sidebar — every claim shows a live status timeline from intimation through settlement.

Can I update my KYC details here? ＋

Yes — KYC uploads are part of the buy/renewal journey and sync automatically with your profile.

What happens after I upload claim photos? ＋

Our AI damage-assessment engine reviews the images and returns an indicative estimate in seconds, before a surveyor confirms it.

Is this portal the same as the app?

This web portal and the Universal Sompo app share the same account and policy data, so you can start a journey on one and finish on the other.
